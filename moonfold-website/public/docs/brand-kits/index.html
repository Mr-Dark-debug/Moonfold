Brand kits with Cassandre

Cassandre turns one prompt into a brand kit: a name, a logo set, colors with roles for light and dark, type with a hierarchy, spacing and radius, voice and tone, ready-to-use exports, and a brand book page. Everything it makes is saved in your project under .moonfold/brand/.

Start one

In any thread, start your message with /brand and describe what you are building:

/brand a calm budgeting app for students

You can also type / and choose /brand from the menu. Any agent that has Moonfold's tools can run it. The thread switches to the Cassandre (brand kit) prompt profile, so follow-up messages keep working on the kit. Switch the profile back from the thread menu when you are done.

What it asks you

Cassandre stops and asks with a question card instead of guessing. Answer in the card; the agent continues as soon as you submit.

  • The brief: who it is for, three personality words, competitors to stand apart from, and anything to avoid.
  • How many designs: choose 2 to 5 directions to explore.
  • References: let it search design galleries such as Dribbble, Pinterest and Behance for you, send your own, or skip them. It may ask whether to research more if what it found is thin.
  • The name, when you don't have one, with domain, GitHub and npm availability for each candidate. Availability is not a trademark check; use the search link it gives you.
  • The direction: each design is shown as a picture together with the references it builds on. Pick one, or describe how to mix them.
  • Applying it: at the end, whether to wire the colors and type into your project's code. Nothing in your code changes unless you say yes.

What you get

FolderWhat is in it
brief.md, plan.mdWhat you asked for and the steps it followed
name/Name candidates, availability checks and the decision
references/The references it kept, with screenshots and its notes
directions/Each design direction and its picture
tokens/tokens.json, the single source of truth for colors, type and spacing
color/, type/, voice/Palette and contrast, type scale and roles, tone of voice
logo/, icons/, templates/Logo variants, app icon sizes, social images
fonts/The fonts used, each with its open-source license
exports/CSS variables, a Tailwind theme, SCSS, TypeScript and JSON
guidelines/The brand book page
reviews/Each review round: who scored what, with a glyph per reviewer

Moonfold checks the kit before exporting: text colors must meet WCAG AA contrast, logos must be plain SVG, and every font needs an open-source license. exports/ is generated from tokens.json, so edit the tokens and ask for a new export instead of editing the exports.

Good to know

  • References are for inspiration only. Cassandre does not copy, trace or imitate them, and the references/ folder is kept out of git because the screenshots belong to their authors.
  • Searching galleries uses Moonfold's browser and its agent browsing rules. Some sites, Pinterest among them, do not allow automated browsing in their terms; block them there if you prefer to collect references yourself.
  • A kit can be resumed later, or continued by another agent after a usage limit. Its progress is in .moonfold/brand/brand.json.
