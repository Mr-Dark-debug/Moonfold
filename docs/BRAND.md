# Moonfold brand

Status: direction chosen in Phase 0 (2026-10-16). The full icon set and wiring into the apps are
Phase 1 work. The owner can override the choice; the concepts stay in the repo until then.

## Mark: the ringed moon

Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-20 from their own reference image, replacing the Phase 0 folded
moon. A crescent moon sits inside a tilted orbit ring, with a four-point star where the ring
swings out at the upper right: a moon that keeps moving, as a task keeps moving between agents.

Geometry lives in one model, `scripts/lib/brand-mark.ts`, in units of the moon's radius R:

- The crescent is the moon disc minus a shadow disc of radius 0.745 R offset to the upper right.
- The ring is an ellipse 1.5 R by 0.3 R, tilted -21.6 degrees. Its back half passes behind the
  moon and its front half crosses the crescent.
- The star has spikes of 0.44 R (vertical) and 0.29 R (horizontal) and sits on the ring.

Variants, all generated by `node scripts/render-brand-icons.ts`:

| Variant                                             | Use                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Full colour (`colourMark`)                          | App icons from 64 px, macOS, iOS, Icon Composer, social image                                          |
| Small (`smallMark`)                                 | Favicons and icon sizes up to 48 px: flat colours, a heavier ring, no star; 16 px gets a larger mark   |
| One colour (`monoMark`)                             | Templates, light or dark surfaces, the docs site; the ring is cut out of the crescent where it crosses |
| In-app (`apps/web/src/components/MoonfoldMark.tsx`) | `currentColor` version with a heavier ring that holds at 16 px                                         |

## The living mark

One character, and it is the mark (D-67): the crescent, ring and star given five states by
motion. Idle rests; working sends the star round the ring; waiting breathes, with two dots by the
parked star; done sparkles once; attention levels the ring and hollows the star. Each state also
has a still pose for reduced motion. Reviewers in a review round get council glyphs (a moon
phase and a star position per seat), and skills get one glyph per source kind. There is no
mascot per feature or per skill, and no provider logos.

Everything is generated from `livingMarkData()` in `scripts/lib/brand-mark.ts`: the data the
apps import (`packages/shared/src/livingMark.generated.json`) and still SVGs for the docs site in
`assets/brand/living/`. The component is `apps/web/src/components/brand/LivingMark.tsx`. QA:
[QA-MASCOT.md](QA-MASCOT.md).
